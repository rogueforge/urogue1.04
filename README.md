UltraRogue is a dungeon game in the line of Rogue, written by Herb
Chong. You choose one of ten character classes, from fighter to ninja,
and go down through a hundred levels of rooms, mazes and throne rooms,
past trading posts and monsters that cast spells and summon others, to
bring back the Amulet of Yendor and the other artifacts.

This is UltraRogue 1.04, dated January 20, 1991.

What is different from 1.03:

- The hero can wear five rings on each hand; known cursed rings are
  marked.
- New monsters: young, old and very old dragons and the achaierai.
  Monster levels and stats are reworked, and more monsters summon help.
- Monster bolts can be dodged, can hit other monsters and grow with the
  monster's level.
- Zapping asks which stick; staffs do more damage than wands.
- Backstabs do (level/4 + 2) times the damage.
- New chances and prices for magic items; enchanting is riskier.
- A deaf hero misses what is only heard, a blind one what is only seen.
- Druids can use silver weapons; c casts a spell and C calls an item.
